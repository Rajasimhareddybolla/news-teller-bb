**Overview:**
Fireblocks, a crypto custodian technology provider, has announced support for Soneium, Sony's Ethereum layer-2 blockchain developed in collaboration with Startale Labs. This support is a crucial step towards enabling institutions to offer custody services for digital assets on the Soneium network. **Key Points:**

*   **Fireblocks' Role:** Fireblocks provides technology that allows institutions, including large banks, to securely store digital assets. This is vital for attracting institutional investment in crypto, as large capital allocators often require custody solutions for insurance and security purposes. [Source: Fireblocks Adds Support for Sony's Soneium, Author: Sam Reynolds]

*   **Soneium Blockchain:** Soneium is a joint venture between Sony and Singapore's Startale Labs. It's an Ethereum layer-2 network aiming to bridge Web2 and Web3. Launched in January, it currently has $33.6 million in Total Value Locked (TVL). [Source: Fireblocks Adds Support for Sony's Soneium, Author: Sam Reynolds]

*   **Soneium's Technology:** Soneium is built using Optimism’s OP Stack and is designed to support applications in gaming, finance, and entertainment. [Source: Fireblocks Adds Support for Sony's Soneium, Author: Sam Reynolds]

*   **Custody Services:** Fireblocks' support for Soneium paves the way for institutions to provide custody services on the new blockchain. [Source: Fireblocks Adds Support for Sony's Soneium, Author: Sam Reynolds]

[Source: Fireblocks Adds Support for Sony's Soneium, Author: Sam Reynolds]

**Context Enhancement:**

*   **Ethereum Layer-2 Networks:** Ethereum layer-2 networks like Soneium are designed to improve the scalability and efficiency of the Ethereum blockchain. They process transactions off-chain and then bundle them onto the main Ethereum chain, reducing congestion and transaction fees. Optimism's OP Stack is a popular framework for building such layer-2 solutions.

*   **Web2 and Web3:** Web2 refers to the current iteration of the internet, characterized by centralized platforms and user data ownership by corporations. Web3 envisions a decentralized internet built on blockchain technology, where users have more control over their data and digital assets.

*   **Total Value Locked (TVL):** TVL is a metric used to measure the value of assets deposited in a decentralized finance (DeFi) protocol or blockchain network. It's an indicator of the platform's popularity and user confidence.

Fireblocks' support for Sony's Soneium blockchain is a significant development for the adoption of this new network. This move underscores the growing interest in layer-2 solutions and the convergence of traditional technology companies like Sony with the blockchain space.
